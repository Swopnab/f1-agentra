04 / CLEAR ANSWERS

The details matter.

Is this a live trading platform?+

No. Signalroom is an independent interface concept and browser-only educational demo. It has no brokerage integration, live data feed, account access, trade execution, or approval service.

Are the demo scores and charts real results?+

No. All companies, symbols, paths, evidence packets, scores, and outcomes are fictional design fixtures. They are not actual trades, historical prices, validated forecasts, or backtest results. Scores do not represent probabilities.

Does “student-first” mean F‑1 compliant?+

No. The audience label is not an immigration or tax determination. A disclaimer, research workflow, or trading-frequency limit does not establish legal eligibility. Consult qualified professionals about your own circumstances.
