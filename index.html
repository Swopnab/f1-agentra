Can I use F1 Agentra to trade real money?+

No. This website is a browser-only educational concept, not a brokerage platform, investment adviser, live agent, or execution service. Everyone may explore the demo; that does not establish eligibility for real trading.

What is real in the interactive workspace?+

The interface interactions and calculation are real software. Every company, symbol, score, indexed path, evidence item, and journal decision is fictional. None represents actual research, trades, historical prices, returns, or backtesting.

Does the F‑1 name mean legally compliant?+

No. It describes the concept’s original student audience, not an immigration or tax determination. Disclaimers and trading-frequency limits do not guarantee eligibility. Seek qualified, individualized guidance before any real activity.
